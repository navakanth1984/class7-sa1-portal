## SECTION C: Picture-Based Experimental Analysis (15 Marks)

### Question 32: Crystallisation Process (5 Marks)

(a) Which separation process is demonstrated when dissolved solid forms geometric shapes upon cooling? (2 Marks)  
(b) Is this process a physical or chemical change? Justify your answer. (3 Marks)

### Question 33: Lime Water Test Experiments (5 Marks)
Four test tube setups (a, b, c, d) were connected via delivery tubes to lime water:
- Setup (a): Vinegar + Baking Soda
- Setup (b): Lemon Juice + Vinegar
- Setup (c): Vinegar + Common Salt
- Setup (d): Lemon Juice + Baking Soda
(a) In which setup(s) does lime water turn milky? (2 Marks)  
(b) Write the chemical name and formula of the gas evolved that causes lime water to turn milky. (1 Mark)  
(c) Write the chemical equation explaining why the lime water becomes milky. (2 Marks)

## SECTION E: Competency-Based Case Study & HOTS (15 Marks)

### Question 38: Case Study 1 — Decomposition & Weathering (5 Marks)
*A wooden log was left outside in the rain and gradually began to rot as fungi and bacteria decomposed the organic material.*  
(a) Identify the type of change (physical or chemical) occurring in the decaying log. Explain why. (2 Marks)  
(b) Natural agents like freezing water in rock crevices crack rocks into soil. Is soil formation purely a physical change or does it involve chemical weathering? Explain. (3 Marks)

### Question 39: Assertion & Reason Analysis (4 Marks)
*Choose: (A) Both A and R true, R correct explanation; (B) Both true, R not correct explanation; (C) A true, R false; (D) Both false.*  
(a) **Assertion (A):** Rusting of iron is a chemical change.  
    **Reason (R):** A new substance is formed when iron reacts with oxygen and water. (2 Marks)  
(b) **Assertion (A):** Adolescence is a phase of rapid physical growth and change.  
    **Reason (R):** Hormones remain inactive during adolescence, affecting physical growth. (2 Marks)
